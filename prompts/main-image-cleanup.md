# Main Image Cleanup Prompt

Use the real product photo at `[INPUT_PATH]`.

Create an Amazon main image:

- Remove the background cleanly.
- Preserve the real product exactly.
- Place the product on a pure white background.
- Center the product.
- Scale the product to fill the frame appropriately, targeting about 85 percent when the product shape allows.
- Export to `[OUTPUT_PATH]` as a 2000 x 2000 JPG or PNG.

Hard rules:

- No text.
- No icons.
- No badges.
- No props.
- No lifestyle scene.
- No fake product features.
- Keep the original file untouched.
