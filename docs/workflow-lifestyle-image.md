# Workflow: Lifestyle Image

Goal: place the real product into a believable secondary/lifestyle scene.

## Inputs

- Real product cutout.
- Approved use case.
- Approved background or local AI-generated background.

## Steps

1. Build or select the background separately from the product.
2. Composite the real product cutout into the scene.
3. Match perspective, lighting, and shadow.
4. Avoid impossible scale or fake product variants.
5. Export a 2000 x 2000 image to `assets/output/`.

## AI Boundary

AI-generated backgrounds are allowed only for secondary images. The product itself must remain based on the real product cutout.
