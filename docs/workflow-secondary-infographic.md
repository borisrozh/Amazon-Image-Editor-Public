# Workflow: Secondary Benefit Infographic

Goal: create a premium secondary image with a real product cutout and a small number of readable benefit callouts.

## Inputs

- Real product cutout in `assets/product-cutouts/`.
- Approved benefit claims.
- Optional background or template asset.

## Steps

1. Confirm every benefit claim is supported.
2. Place the real product cutout as the visual anchor.
3. Add one mobile-readable headline.
4. Add 3 to 5 benefit callouts maximum.
5. Keep hierarchy clean: headline, product, callouts, small supporting details.
6. Export a square 2000 x 2000 image to `assets/output/`.
7. Confirm text is readable at mobile size.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent claims.
- Do not modify product labels or logos misleadingly.
- Do not use tiny cluttered callouts.
