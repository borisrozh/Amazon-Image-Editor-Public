# Amazon Image Rules

This project is built for product-accurate Amazon listing images. When in doubt, preserve the real product and avoid claims that cannot be proven.

## Main Image

- Use a real product photo.
- Use a pure white background.
- Do not add text, icons, graphics, badges, comparison labels, props, hands, lifestyle scenes, or decorative context.
- Do not create a fake product with AI generation.
- Center the product and fill the frame appropriately, usually around 85 percent when the product shape allows.
- Export a clean square image, usually 2000 x 2000 pixels.

## Secondary Images

- Use real product cutouts as the product source.
- Benefit callouts, dimensions, comparison graphics, and lifestyle scenes are allowed.
- Keep headlines mobile-readable.
- Use 3 to 5 callouts at most.
- Avoid clutter and tiny labels.
- Do not invent features, quantities, certifications, materials, warranty claims, or compatibility.

## Lifestyle Images

- AI-generated or designed backgrounds are allowed only for secondary/lifestyle images.
- The product must remain based on the real product cutout.
- Match lighting, shadow, and perspective honestly.
- Do not show impossible use cases, unsafe use, fake accessories, or exaggerated scale.

## Verification

Before using an output, check:

- Dimensions are Amazon-ready.
- Main image background is likely white.
- Product is not cropped unintentionally.
- Text is absent from main image.
- Secondary image text is readable on mobile.
- File name clearly identifies workflow and version.
